Civic Fleet Services City Data Agreement

This Data Agreement is between Civic Fleet Services LLC, a Utah limited liability company ("Civic Fleet Services"), and [AGENCY NAME] ("City"). It applies whenever the City puts real data into the Civic Fleet Services fleet management software (the "Service") under a signed order form or agreement (the "Signed Agreement"). It is part of the Signed Agreement.

1. Who owns what

  1. City owns its data. All data the City or its users put into the Service, and all reports created from it ("City Data"), belong to the City.
  2. Civic Fleet Services owns the software. Civic Fleet Services LLC owns the Service and all intellectual property in it. The City gets a right to use the Service during the term. No ownership of the software transfers to the City.
  3. Limited use. Civic Fleet Services uses City Data only to provide, support, secure, and back up the Service for the City, or as the law requires. It doesn't sell City Data, share it with other customers, or use it to train outside AI models. Any outside AI provider needs the City's prior written agreement.

2. Term and funding

  1. The term is [1 year / 3 years] from [START DATE], as stated in the Signed Agreement.
  2. Each year depends on funding. Each year after the first depends on the City's governing body approving funding. If funding isn't approved, the City may end the Signed Agreement at the end of the funded year with written notice.
  3. No early-exit penalty. If the City ends the agreement for lack of funding, it owes no fee, penalty, or repayment of credits or discounts. It pays only for service already received.

3. Security

  1. Civic Fleet Services will keep reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards for City Data, as described in its current security policies (attached or linked). It won't materially reduce those safeguards during the term.
  2. Hosting: United States only (currently DigitalOcean). Each City's data is kept separate from other customers' data. [Attorney/Dan: confirm the current state of uploaded-file storage and backups before signing.]
  3. Subprocessors: Civic Fleet Services will keep a current list and give the City notice before adding one that will process City Data.

4. Security incidents

  1. If Civic Fleet Services confirms an incident involving unauthorized access to City Data, it will notify the City's named contact within [72] hours of confirming it, and sooner if it can. [Dan to decide the window.]
  2. Civic Fleet Services will cooperate with the City's investigation and any notices the City must give, including under Utah Code 13-44-202 or the City's own state's law.

5. Public records and legal requests

City Data is the City's record. Civic Fleet Services will reasonably help the City respond to public records requests. If Civic Fleet Services gets a subpoena or other legal demand for City Data, it will send the request to the City and tell the City promptly, unless the law forbids that.

6. Return and deletion at the end

  1. Export anytime. The City's Admin can export City Data at any time at no charge, using the export tools in the Service.
  2. After the end date. The City keeps read-only access for [30] days. Within 30 days of the end date, Civic Fleet Services also provides a final full export of City Data (every table as CSV and JSON, plus uploaded files where available).
  3. Deletion. After the City confirms it has its export, or 30 days after the final export is delivered, whichever comes first, Civic Fleet Services deletes City Data from the live system. Deleted data is gone from backups 7 days later. Civic Fleet Services sends the City a written deletion confirmation with the dates.
  4. Legal holds and retention. Civic Fleet Services won't delete City Data while the City has given notice of a legal hold or a records-retention need. The City is responsible for its own records retention once it has its export.

7. Liability, indemnity, and insurance

[DRAFT for attorney review. Insurance limits must match the policies actually bound.]

  1. Civic Fleet Services defends the City. Civic Fleet Services will defend the City and its officers and employees against third-party claims, and pay any resulting judgments or settlements it approves, to the extent the claim arises from:
    a. a claim that the Service, as provided by Civic Fleet Services, infringes a U.S. patent, copyright, or trademark, or misappropriates a trade secret;
    b. a security incident involving City Data caused by Civic Fleet Services' breach of Section 3 or 4; or
    c. bodily injury or damage to tangible property caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Civic Fleet Services or its personnel.
  2. What's excluded. Item 1(a) doesn't cover claims caused by City Data, the City's changes to the Service, or use with things Civic Fleet Services didn't provide. If an infringement claim is made, Civic Fleet Services may change the Service so it doesn't infringe, get the right for the City to keep using it, or end the affected part and refund prepaid fees for the unused period.
  3. How it works. The City notifies Civic Fleet Services promptly in writing. Civic Fleet Services controls the defense and settlement, but no settlement may admit fault for the City or bind the City without its consent. The City may use its own attorney at its own cost. This section doesn't limit the authority of the City's attorney or any official the law gives control of the City's legal matters.
  4. Caps.
    a. Each indemnity in item 1 is capped at the limit of the matching insurance: $1,000,000 per occurrence for 1(c) (general liability), $1,000,000 for 1(b) (cyber), and $2,000,000 for 1(a) (technology errors and omissions), or the actual limits of insurance in force, if higher.
    b. For all other claims, each party's total liability is capped at the greater of the fees the City paid in the 12 months before the claim or $1,000,000.
    c. Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages, or for lost profits, except to the extent those amounts are part of a third-party claim covered by item 1.
    d. The caps don't apply to fraud, willful misconduct, or anything else the law doesn't allow to be limited.
  5. No City indemnity or waiver. The City doesn't indemnify Civic Fleet Services. Nothing here waives the City's immunities, defenses, or notice requirements, including under the Governmental Immunity Act of Utah or the City's own state's law.
  6. Insurance. During the term, Civic Fleet Services will carry: commercial general liability of $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate; technology errors and omissions of $2,000,000; cyber and privacy liability of $1,000,000; and hired and non-owned auto of $1,000,000 if it drives on City business. The City is named as additional insured on the general liability policy, which is primary and non-contributory and includes a waiver of subrogation in the City's favor. Civic Fleet Services gives a certificate of insurance before the City puts real data in the Service and gives 30 days' notice of cancellation. If Civic Fleet Services has no employees, a workers' compensation waiver is provided instead of coverage.
  7. Free trials. For free trials and demo-data pilots, the Terms of Service apply instead, including the $100 cap.

8. Law

Utah law governs, unless the City's own state law requires its own law to apply.
