Replacement scoring and budget

PMs in the asset file

  • Last done, next due and status for each schedule

Work orders that print like an invoice

Mobile DVIR with safety lockout

  • Drivers walk the unit on a phone: OK or Defect per item
  • A safety defect opens a WO right away and marks the unit Out of Service
  • The record shows the defect, the UNSAFE result and the WO it created

No waiting for someone to re-key a paper form.

Tech Entry and time clock

  • Clock in, switch jobs, break or clock out from a phone in the bay

  • One job at a time, so time lands on the right WO line

Time clock and WO labor in one place.
